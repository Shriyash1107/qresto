🎯 QUICK START (2 minutes):

   1. Open http://localhost:5173 in your browser
   
   2. Register an account (any email works - test@demo.com)
   
   3. Create a QR code:
      - Name: My First QR
      - Type: Single Redirect
      - URL: https://google.com
   
   4. Download PNG and scan with phone → Goes to Google ✅
   
   5. Edit QR, change URL to https://github.com
   
   6. Scan SAME QR code again → Goes to GitHub! ✅
      (This proves dynamic redirection works!)
