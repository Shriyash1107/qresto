⚠️  IMPORTANT: URL FORMAT FOR QR CODES

   When creating a QR code, ALWAYS include the protocol:

   ✅ CORRECT:
      https://google.com
      https://www.example.com
      http://example.com

   ❌ WRONG:
      google.com
      www.example.com
      example.com

   Without https:// or http://, the redirect will not work!
